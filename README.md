**Ondaluz Undercurrent** is an original open-world crime game for desktop Chrome, written with three.js and plain ES modules (no build step, no network). Kai Moreno comes home to Ondaluz, a sun-soaked Atlantic coast city of art-deco hotels, glass towers, a market plaza, the Ironwharf docks and Sunwheel Beach, and takes back the docks from Silas Crane over seven story missions. Steal and drive any car with drift-friendly handling, outrun a 1–5 star wanted level with pursuing cruisers, officers on foot and roadblocks, fight with fists, a pistol or an SMG, and fill the days with street races, Sunny Cab fares, Pelican Express deliveries, the Bayside Arms shop and twelve hidden sunshells. The HUD has a rotating minimap with GPS route and blips, wanted stars, money, health and armour, objectives and a speedometer, plus a full-screen map, pause menu, settings, stats, saving to localStorage, and music, radio stations, engine, sirens and effects synthesized with the Web Audio API.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts (then open http://localhost:8080)
```
