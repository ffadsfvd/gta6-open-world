**Ondaluz Undercurrent** is an original open-world crime game for desktop Chrome, written with three.js and plain ES modules (no build step, no network). Kai Moreno comes home to Ondaluz, an Atlantic coast city of streamline hotels, round glass towers, a market plaza, the Ironwharf docks, a marina and Sunwheel Beach, and takes back the docks from Silas Crane over seven story missions, with his contacts calling him on the phone mid-chase and dispatch on his police scanner. A full day passes in 24 minutes: golden-hour sunsets, starry nights with lit windows, street lamps and neon. Steal and drive any of nine rounded, detailed car models; outrun a 1–5 star wanted level where every cruiser, officer on foot and the Skyhawk helicopter has a call sign and a live tactic (chasing, boxing in, ramming, roadblocks, searching your last known position), even when you escape in a stolen cruiser; fight with fists, pistol, shotgun, SMG or marksman rifle (a round in the head drops a thug), dive-roll out of the line of fire, and fill the days with street races, Sunny Cab fares, Pelican Express deliveries, the Bayside Arms shop and twelve hidden sunshells, earning street respect through seven ranks that unlock heavier guns. Pedestrians keep right and step around each other on busy sidewalks. The HUD shows a mission tracker (mission, stage X of Y, objective, next step, live readouts such as fare, tip or race position), the police response, a weapon bar, the car's speed, gear and condition, street rank, the radio's now-playing line, a rotating minimap with GPS route and blips, wanted stars, money, clock, health and armour, laid out to stay clear of each other from 1280x720 to 2560x1440, plus a full-screen map, pause menu, settings, stats, saving to localStorage, and music, radio stations, engine, sirens and effects synthesized with the Web Audio API.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts (then open http://localhost:8080)
```
